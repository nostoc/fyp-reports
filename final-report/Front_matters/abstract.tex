
\chapter*{Abstract}


Local governments in developing countries often
struggle to address public issues efficiently, mainly due to
centralized data control, lack of transparency and insufficient
citizen participation. While crowdsourced reporting systems and
e-governance platforms are designed to encourage citizen engagement, their effectiveness is limited due to concerns related
to data integrity, user anonymity and content reliability. This
paper presents a community-based civic reporting system that
addresses these challenges using a private permissioned Proofof-Authority (PoA) blockchain integrated with off-chain storage,
an Artificial Intelligence (AI) based content moderation service,
smart contracts and a Web3 decentralized application. User
identities are decoupled from the report content using a simulated
identity and role-based access control mechanism to preserve
citizen privacy. This system enables citizens to submit public issue
reports privately and participate in opinion polling. The underlying blockchain ensures data immutability and transparency. To
reduce on-chain storage overhead, reporting data are hosted offchain on InterPlanetary File System (IPFS) with their Content
Identifier (CID) hashes anchored on-chain. An AI based content
moderation service filters text and image submissions to minimize
the impact of malicious, inappropriate content. Performance
evaluation shows the proposed framework offers a practical,
secure, and scalable solution for modern local e-governance.
