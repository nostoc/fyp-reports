
\chapter*{Preface} 

This project report is submitted to the Department of Electrical and Information Engineering, Faculty of Engineering, University of Ruhuna, Sri Lanka, in partial fulfillment of the requirements for the Degree of Bachelor of the Science of Engineering Honours. The work described here represents collaborative research and development tasks carried out by the authors under the supervision of Dr. Subodha Gunawardena. 

The primary motivation of this project originates from the critical privacy, security, and integrity concerns of traditional centralized e-governance systems. To overcome the aforementioned issues, a novel blockchain-based, community-assisted, privacy-preserving reporting framework is presented. The prototype integrates a private permissioned \ac{PoA} blockchain, \ac{IPFS}, smart contracts, and an \ac{AI}-based content moderation service. The identity of citizens is decoupled from their reports by using government-verified ticket-based nullifiers. The identity service is simulated for this prototype. To demonstrate the feasibility of the framework,  a fully responsive, web-based \ac{DApp} has been developed and evaluated within a controlled environment. The results and findings presented in this report provide a verified, extensible foundation for modern, transparent, and participatory e-governance.
